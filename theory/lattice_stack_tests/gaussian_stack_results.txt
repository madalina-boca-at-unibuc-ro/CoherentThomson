How to read it:
- 1: sigma_z = 0 leaves every output byte-identical, including electron_beam_scatter.dat (its w column appears only
  when the charges differ), and a random beam ignores the stack keys.
- 2: "charges vs formula" is the largest |w - formula| / max w over the sheets, from electron_beam_scatter.dat (6
  printed digits, hence ~1e-7); "z" the largest sheet-position error in lambda_1. The log's sum matches
  sinc(qH/2) exp(-q^2 sigma_z^2/2) to the tail cut's ~1e-9 or better.
- 3: absolute |c - conj(sum)| sits at the control's level (1e-7 to 1e-9, the off-axis variation of q on the screen).
  Where |b| is ~1e-9 (H1.0_sigma0.3, N = 2 of H0.0_sigma0.5) that floor is the whole field, hence the large eta.
- 4: modulated stacks match a fine quadrature of the moved continuous density to ~1e-8 at 60 to 120 sheets.
